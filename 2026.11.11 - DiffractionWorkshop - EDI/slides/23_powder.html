Results stay linked to their models and measured data.

Current analysis workflow, grouped from calculation and fitting to inspection and saved results. Free/fixed parameter selection, constraints and weighted least squares are supported. Single and joint fits cover individual and combined experiments; sequential and independent scans cover measurement series. Inspect residuals, parameter uncertainties, correlations and evolution. Interactive maps, stacked patterns and structure views are application capabilities; notebooks and reports offer the views documented for their interface, not identical controls everywhere. Projects retain the analysis, with HTML/PDF/CIF reports for results. PDF export is available in the application; HTML/CIF are available across interfaces. No claim of current Bayesian sampling or PDF is made. Source: https://enhantica.github.io/edi/user-guide/features/
