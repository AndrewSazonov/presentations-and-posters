Different libraries for different analysis methods.

code.ill.fr/scientific-software/crysfml · cryspy.fr · diffpy.org. CrysFML is the most mature, with the widest coverage (symmetry, magnetic structures), CrysPy has polarised-neutron analysis and site susceptibility (needed for MAGIC), PDFfit2 the pair distribution function. Each library is maintained by its own experts; EasyDiffraction gives them one interface.
