What the ESS instruments will ask of the analysis: large series, up to a thousand datasets of a hundred patterns each; multidimensional data and very large parameter spaces; and automation, where a bad dataset must not stop the run. The engines EasyDiffraction uses today work, but Python wrappers and numerical derivatives make them too slow for this.
