Current status in the full feature catalogue.

The forthcoming feature catalogue distinguishes available, partial, work in progress, planned and proposed. This slide gives selected examples only. MCMC sampling is planned for Python, proposed for app/CLI. Ikeda–Carpenter and instrument-resolution separation are planned. Magnetic manual creation remains work in progress.
