A possible future: alongside existing libraries.

crysta is a C++ crystallographic calculation and refinement engine with Python bindings: Eigen for the linear algebra, nanobind for the bindings, CMake to build it. It does not replace the picture from before; it slots into it, in both engine groups at once, under the same EasyDiffraction interface.
