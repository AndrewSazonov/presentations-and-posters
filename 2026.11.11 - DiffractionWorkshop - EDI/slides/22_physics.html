Broad coverage for crystal and magnetic structure refinement.

Current crysta/edi coverage, grouped by the data and model rather than by individual parameters. Powder: CW and TOF neutrons, X-rays. Single crystal: neutron diffraction, including extinction models; this is not a claim of X-ray single-crystal support. Crystal models include symmetry, atomic positions, occupancies and isotropic/anisotropic displacement parameters. Commensurate magnetic structures are available. Multiple phases and measured datasets share the project; scales and derived mass fractions are supported. Peak profiles, instrument calibration, backgrounds, absorption and preferred-orientation corrections are included. PDF and Bayesian capabilities from the earlier library belong to the porting roadmap, not this current-coverage list. Source: https://enhantica.github.io/edi/user-guide/features/
