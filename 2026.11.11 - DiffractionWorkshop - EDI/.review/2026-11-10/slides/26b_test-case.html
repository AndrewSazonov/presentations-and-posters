A selected benchmark for crystal structure refinement.

Everything that follows is this one case: the WISH banks 2 and 9 time-of-flight pattern of the NCAF standard, refined from one declared start with the same 48 free parameters in every engine. FullProf is the reference. The machine is a VISA node. Reference timings use one thread; crysta is shown with one and six threads.
