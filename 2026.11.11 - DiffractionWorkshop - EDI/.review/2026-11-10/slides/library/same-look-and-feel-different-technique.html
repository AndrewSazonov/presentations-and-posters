Say the comparison out loud rather than listing it on the slide: SHARED — the workflow bar, the zones, the parameter table, the fit button. TECHNIQUE-SPECIFIC — the plot, the model, the parameter names. Three screenshots side by side make the point faster than lines of text do. All are captures of the current applications, at the same size, so the comparison is fair and not an artefact of when each was taken. If anyone asks about "Model" versus "Sample": that is the point, not a slip. The tab bar has the same six positions in EasyDiffraction and EasyReflectometry, and the third one carries the word that technique uses for the thing being fitted. The skeleton is shared; the vocabulary is not.
