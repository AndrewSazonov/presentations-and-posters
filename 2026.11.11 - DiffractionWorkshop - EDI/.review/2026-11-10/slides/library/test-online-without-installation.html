Try EasyDiffraction in your browser.

Two ways to try EasyDiffraction without a local installation. On the left, open a Jupyter tutorial from the documentation in Google Colab. These use the pre-crysta library. On the right, open the crysta-based web app and select an example project. Counts describe these content collections, not a complete list of supported instruments.
