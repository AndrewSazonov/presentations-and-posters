Back to diffraction: what analysis needs

Large series · 1,000 datasets × 100 patterns temperature, field, time

Multidimensional data · huge parameter spaces 2D and 3D detectors

Automation · experiments that run themselves no human in the loop

EasyDiffraction works today, but not fast enough.

Back to diffraction. What the ESS instruments will ask of the analysis: large series, up to a thousand datasets of a hundred patterns each; multidimensional data and very large parameter spaces; and automation, where a bad dataset must not stop the run. The engines EasyDiffraction uses today work, but Python wrappers and numerical derivatives make them too slow for this.
