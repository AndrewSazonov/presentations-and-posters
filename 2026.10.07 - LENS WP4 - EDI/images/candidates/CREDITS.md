# Image candidates

Alternatives for every slide whose photograph I sourced myself. Nothing here is used by the deck:
`<slot>-<n>.jpg` is cropped to the deck's 1600×900, so adopting one is a copy over the current
file; `sheet-<slot>.jpg` is the numbered contact sheet shown in chat.

Everything is from Wikimedia Commons unless noted. **A chosen image's line must move into the
appendix credits slide** — several are CC BY-SA, which the current credits line does not cover.
