It also mixed up two ways of measuring a peak’s width the width at half height is 2.35× the Gaussian σ — the formula wanted σ

Both were found by tests written before the fix

crysta 5e3197f4, ADR-0042, 3 July. The Jorgensen–von Dreele time-of-flight peak shape contains exp(u)·erfc(y); written straight from the paper, exp(u) overflows to infinity while erfc(y) underflows to zero, so the product is inf×0 = NaN across the peak tails. Evaluating it as exp(u + log erfc y) — the Mantid form — never forms the overflowing exponential and the slow-beta tail keeps its true small value. Two more in the same function: the Gaussian kernel quantity was labelled the FWHM when it was the standard deviation (they differ by sqrt(8 ln 2) = 2.355), and the Thompson-Cox-Hastings seam combined the wrong two quantities. The commit trail shows the test lane authoring the gamma-not-zero golden and the overflow regression BEFORE the implement lane fixed it.
