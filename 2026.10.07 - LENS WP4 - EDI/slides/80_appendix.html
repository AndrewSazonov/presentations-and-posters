Rules for questions: (a, a′) × (b, b′) = (a·b, a′·b + a·b′) is the product rule; exp((a, a′)) = (exp a, exp a · a′) is the chain rule. Seed the parameter of interest with slope 1 and everything else with slope 0. AD agrees with central finite differences to 1.3e-6 on every column.

Real rows carry a UTC timestamp and the commit sha, e.g. "r12 2026-08-30T14:02Z implement: green @a3f9c12 — hidden 37/37". A message to an agent is a sequencing instruction, never an authority: an agent refuses any claim the ledger does not show; six forged messages were caught this way. That third step runs again and again — and that is where the time goes

One refinement evaluates the whole pattern once per iteration, and a derivative-based minimiser needs it many times per iteration. So the cost of a refinement is the cost of one pattern calculation multiplied by a few hundred — which is why the rest of this talk is about how fast and how exactly that one calculation can be done.
