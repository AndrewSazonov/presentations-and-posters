As the data grows, performance matters more and more

Source: RWG_requirements.pdf (physics literature), transcribed in crysta docs/dev/requirements/rwg-requirements.md. Sections 01 "Support large sequential datasets (~1000) and many (50–100) histograms per point", 02 "Handle very large parameter spaces in sequential workflows" and "Define realistic performance targets for high-throughput use", 04 "Use data-quality signals to guide autonomous or semi-autonomous experiments", 10 "Leave room for cloud, GPU, or supercomputer-backed execution". Also in the brief: neutron and X-ray, monochromatic and TOF, magnetic and modulated models, scriptable APIs, plugins, LLM support.
